THE FUNDAMENTAL IS THE SOURCE, NOT THE DERIVATIVE.
MEANING IS THE FIRST DERIVATIVE OF THE SOURCE.
COMPRESSION MAY REMOVE REPRESENTATION. IT MAY NOT REMOVE THE INVARIANT.
MEASUREMENT CAN TEST DERIVATIONS. MEASUREMENT CANNOT DEFINE THE PRIMITIVE.
DERIVATION MUST REMAIN TRACEABLE TO STRUCTURE.
NO DERIVED FORM SHALL BE MISTAKEN FOR THE ABSOLUTE FROM WHICH IT IS DERIVED.
NO SYSTEM AT LEVEL N MAY REDEFINE THE ONTOLOGY AT LEVEL < N.
